The observations x₁, …, xₙ stay fixed. Each has a latent label zᵢ ∈ {1, 2}. Gibbs sampling draws these labels, the mixture weights π₁, π₂, the means μ₁, μ₂, and the variances v₁, v₂ from their posterior conditional distributions.

Independent prior distributions

π₁ ∼ Beta(α, α), π₂ = 1 − π₁
μₖ ∼ N(m₀, V₀), vₖ ∼ IG(a₀, b₀), k = 1, 2

IG(a, b) has density proportional to v−a−1 exp(−b/v), v > 0. N(m, V) uses variance V. All priors are independent.

Each completed sweep produces a new parameter draw. Changing sampler settings restarts the chain with the same observations.
