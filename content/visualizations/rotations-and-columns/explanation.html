Let W be the diagonal matrix with entries 2 and 1, and let R be a rotation matrix. The columns of W are perpendicular, with lengths 2 and 1.

Left multiplication, RW: rotate both columns by the same angle. Their lengths and their 90° angle stay the same.

Right multiplication, WR: the new columns are combinations of the original columns. Equivalently, rotate the coordinate unit vectors first, then stretch them using W. This unequal stretching can change their angle.

At a 45° rotation, the columns of WR have inner product −1.5 and form an angle of about 126.9°. Select “Equal initial column lengths” to see why equal scaling preserves the right angle.
